## 1. The destroyer (Richard Wells Thompson Jr., b. 10 Feb 1926, Wichita Falls)

He graduated from Wichita Falls Senior High in May 1943, so he probably
enlisted between mid-1943 and 1944.

- **U.S., World War II Navy Muster Rolls, 1938–1949.** Search "Richard W
  Thompson" / "Richard Wells Thompson". The muster roll shows the ship (look for
  a "DD-" hull number), his service number, rate and dates aboard. Record every
  ship he appears on.
- **U.S., WWII Draft Cards Young Men, 1940–1947.** He turned 18 in Feb 1944.
  The card gives his address, employer, and a "person who will always know your
  address" (often a parent), which may give Rubye's full name.
- **U.S., Department of Veterans Affairs BIRLS Death File.** Enlistment and
  release dates.
- Once the ship is known, the Naval History and Heritage Command's DANFS entry
  for it is free and citable for its WWII service.

## 2. Rubye and Richard W. Thompson Sr. (Wichita Falls, 1926)

- **1930 and 1940 U.S. Census**, Wichita Falls, Wichita Co., Texas: a
  household with Richard W. Thompson, wife Rubye and son Richard (b. ~1926).
  This gives ages, birthplaces and occupations.
- **U.S., Social Security Applications and Claims Index** for Richard Wells
  Thompson Jr. It usually lists father's and mother's full names, including
  the mother's maiden name.
- **Texas, Birth Certificates** (Richard Jr., Feb 1926, Wichita Co.): parents'
  names, ages and birthplaces.
- **Texas, Death Certificates** and **Find a Grave** for Richard Sr. and Rubye.
  Their death certificates name their own parents: the next generation.
- **Texas, Select County Marriage Records**: Richard W. Thompson and Rubye,
  about 1920–1925.

## 3. Euvonne Roberson's parents (Elk City, Oklahoma)

The working hypothesis (Tentative) is that she was the daughter of Mack
Roberson (b. ~1890 Arkansas, widowed by 1950, 910 W. Avenue A, Elk City).

- **1940 and 1930 U.S. Census**, Elk City, Beckham Co., Oklahoma: is there a
  household with Mack Roberson, a wife, Euvonne (b. ~1927), Bob or Robert and
  Billy J.? That would confirm the link and name her mother.
- **U.S., Social Security Applications and Claims Index** for Euvonne Roberson
  Thompson: her parents' names.
- **Oklahoma death records / Find a Grave** for Mrs. Mack Roberson (died before
  1950) and Mack himself.
- **Newspapers.com**: "Euvonne" in the *Elk City Daily News* (1945–1955). Look
  for a 1951 wedding write-up naming her parents and Richard's parents, and for
  her mother's obituary.
